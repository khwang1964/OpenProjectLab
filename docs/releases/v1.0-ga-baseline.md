---

## 1. Purpose

GA.1 establishes the reviewed baseline from which OpenProjectLab may later
decide whether to transition from the accepted Release Candidate to stable
`v1.0.0`.

GA.1 does **not** authorize the GA version transition, GA tag creation,
artifact publication, GitHub Release creation, or formal GA Acceptance.

The purpose of this step is to make the RC-to-GA decision evidence-first and
fail-closed.

## 8. GA Finding Classification

Every RC-period finding must be assigned exactly one reviewed disposition:

```text
GA blocker
GA correction
Post-GA patch candidate
v1.1+ deferred improvement
```

Definitions:

### GA blocker

A defect that violates the stable v1.0 promise and must be corrected before
GA.

### GA correction

A correctness, compatibility, documentation, packaging, security, or release
automation correction accepted into the GA source before publication.

### Post-GA patch candidate

A non-blocking correction that may be delivered in `v1.0.x` only if the GA
contract remains satisfied without it.

### v1.1+ deferred improvement

Useful work outside the accepted v1.0 GA scope.

- RC publication identity is verified unchanged;
- RC terminal acceptance provenance is recorded;
- GA governing-contract provenance is recorded;
- repository version remains `1.0.0rc1`;
- actual RC-period evidence has been reviewed;
- every finding is classified;
- unresolved GA blockers are explicit;
- any GA correction is explicitly identified;
- no missing evidence is converted into an assumed pass;
- GA version/tag/publication remain unauthorized until reviewed exit.
